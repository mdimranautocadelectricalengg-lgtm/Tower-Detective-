# 5G NR

5G NR is the radio technology used by 5G networks.

Android may expose information such as:

- NCI
- TAC
- PCI
- MCC
- MNC
- signal strength

NCI is a 5G NR cell identity value.

The presence of 5G information does not by itself provide
the physical address of the cellular tower.

Carrier and device behavior can affect the information returned.
