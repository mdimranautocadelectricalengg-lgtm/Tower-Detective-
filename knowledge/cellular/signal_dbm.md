# Cellular Signal dBm

dBm is a logarithmic measurement of received radio signal power.

General rough interpretation:

- Around -50 dBm: very strong
- Around -70 dBm: strong
- Around -85 dBm: good
- Around -95 dBm: moderate
- Around -105 dBm: weak
- Around -115 dBm: very weak

These values are approximate.

Signal strength alone does not determine internet speed.

Network technology, bandwidth, congestion, interference,
device capability and other radio conditions also affect performance.

Never use dBm alone to calculate the physical distance to a tower.
